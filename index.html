01 / Research tooling + educational demo

Graph-EVT-agent

When did an extreme event begin, and which channel responded first? A Python pipeline separates detection, graph construction and source ranking.

Method
POT/GPD tail modelling, AR / DFA / Kuramoto-wavelet graphs, transparent ranking and optional trained GNN/GAT models.
Explore
The educational companion includes 1-D and multichannel notebooks. Its browser lab lets you change a 12-oscillator Kuramoto system and watch an event propagate.

The teaching demo uses synthetic data. Dependence and source rankings do not establish causality or clinical validity.

Pipeline code ↗ Educational notebooks ↗ Open interactive guide ↗ Open propagation lab ↗

02 / Interactive lab · EN / RU

Reservoir Computing

Where does a model keep its memory? Compare a feed-forward network with an echo state network, then follow a forecast through the computation.

Try it
Change the spectral radius and leak rate; inspect a 100-neuron reservoir and compare forecasting baselines.
Go deeper
Connect reservoir dynamics to state-space control, impulse responses and reduced-order models. Companion NumPy experiments reproduce the lab.

03 / Interactive lab · EN / RU

Embeddings & Attention

Follow a sentence into GPT-2: from byte-pair tokenization and token IDs to embeddings, positions and the first self-attention layer.

Try it
Inspect BPE merges, train a tiny embedding model and trace Q/K/V, masking and attention weights.
Under the hood
The explainer uses GPT-2's tokenizer and selected real model weights. Its verification script compares the computations with Transformers.

Attention views download model weights on first use; tokenization can run without those downloads.

Open attention lab ↗ Code & methods ↗

04 / Interactive lab · EN / RU

Autoencoders & Forecasting

Explore what a compressed representation keeps—and loses. Move from a one-dimensional bottleneck to digit embeddings, VAEs and time-series forecasts.

Try it
Train a toy autoencoder, draw a digit, vary the VAE's β parameter and inspect possible futures.
Go deeper
Compare forecasting autoencoders with NLinear on ETTh1 and examine uncertainty through a conditional VAE.
